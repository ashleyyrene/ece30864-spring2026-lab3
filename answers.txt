(a) Pros: There is constant collaboration with customers. It is broken down into stages for implementing and testing.

Cons: Requirements may not be fixed so stages could be more tedious to plan. Could take longer to implement since customers can make changes. 

Non-agile development can be riskier and less efficient. No value to the customer until the end.

(b) Scrum Master: Handles all scrum related tasks including Jira tooling, facilitating sprints and meetings, etc.

Product Owner: Handles releases and stakeholder management as well as sets direction for the team.
